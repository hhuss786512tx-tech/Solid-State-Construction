Our Turn-key Services

  • Design-Build

    Offers a fast, efficient, and cost-effective approach by combining design and construction under a single point of contact, streamlining communication and project timelines.

  • Design-Bid-Build

    DBB is a linear process where design, bidding, and construction occur sequentially, offering reliable budgets but limited flexibility and high costs for later changes.

  • Construction Management at Risk

    CMAR mirrors DBB but adds an advisory role and the ability to overlap steps, offering a collaborative design process and faster construction start, with the risk of a Guaranteed Maximum Price instead of a Fixed Price.

  • Integrated Project Delivery

    IPD fosters collaboration by aligning incentives and goals among all parties, enhancing project success but requiring careful team selection to mitigate adversarial risks.
