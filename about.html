Our Story

From Concrete Specialist to Full-Service Contractor

Solid State Construction was founded in 2011 in Austin, Texas, with a single crew and a single specialty: concrete. Foundations, driveways, flatwork — we built a reputation one pour at a time, on the belief that quality craftsmanship and straight talk with customers would earn repeat business faster than any ad campaign could.

As that reputation grew, so did the requests. Clients who trusted us with their concrete started asking if we could handle their plumbing, their roofing, their underground utility work. Rather than refer that work out, we built the capability in-house — hiring licensed tradespeople, investing in equipment, and holding every new service line to the same standard as our first one.

Today, Solid State Construction is a full-service general contractor licensed and insured to handle concrete, tunneling, plumbing, roofing, and emergency water damage remediation across Austin and the greater Central Texas metro — Round Rock, Cedar Park, Georgetown, San Marcos, and Pflugerville. One contractor, one point of contact, start to finish.
