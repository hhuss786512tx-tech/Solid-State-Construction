Our Process

From Flooded to Fully Restored

A proven five-step process built to minimize damage, prevent mold, and get your property — and your life — back to normal.

01

Inspection & Assessment

We arrive fast to assess the source, scope, and category of water damage using moisture-detection equipment.

02

Water Extraction

Industrial pumps and vacuums remove standing water quickly to limit structural damage and contamination.

03

Drying & Dehumidification

Commercial-grade air movers and dehumidifiers dry out structure, flooring, and drywall completely.

04

Mold Prevention

Antimicrobial treatment and continuous monitoring stop mold growth before it starts.

05

Repair & Restoration

Our in-house crews handle drywall, flooring, and finish repairs — one contractor, start to finish.
