How It Works

From Estimate to Finished Slab

A straightforward process built around clear pricing, clean job sites, and concrete that lasts decades in the Central Texas climate.

01

Site Assessment & Free Estimate

We evaluate soil conditions, drainage, and load requirements, then provide a transparent, itemized quote.

02

Excavation & Formwork

Precise grading and forming ensures proper slope, thickness, and drainage before a single yard of concrete is poured.

03

Reinforcement & Pour

Rebar or wire mesh reinforcement, then a clean, properly vibrated pour to eliminate air pockets and weak spots.

04

Finishing & Curing

Broom, stamped, or exposed-aggregate finishing, sealed and cured properly for maximum durability and color hold.
